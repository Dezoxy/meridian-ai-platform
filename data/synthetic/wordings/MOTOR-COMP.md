# Meridian Insurance — Motor Comprehensive

Product code: `MOTOR-COMP`. Wording version: 2026-01.

This document is synthetic and fictional. Meridian Insurance is an invented
insurer, and nothing in this wording is a real contract or real cover.

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 You and we

In this wording "you" means the policyholder named on the schedule, and "we"
means Meridian Insurance. The schedule is the document that shows your product,
your dates, your deductible and your sum insured or limit. It forms part of this
wording.

### 1.2 The insured vehicle

The insured vehicle is the car described on the schedule by make, model, year
and registration. It includes the parts and accessories fitted to it by the
manufacturer and any others listed on the schedule.

### 1.3 Deductible

The deductible is the part of each claim that you pay yourself. We take it off
the amount we would otherwise pay, as clause 4.1 explains.

### 1.4 Sum insured

The sum insured is the value of the insured vehicle that is shown on the
schedule. It is the most we pay for one claim under this product.

### 1.5 Period of cover

The period of cover is the time from the start date to the end date shown on the
schedule, both days included. Clause 6.1 explains it in full.

## 2. What is covered

The clauses below list what Motor Comprehensive covers. Each clause names one
event. We pay for a covered event up to the limit in clause 4.2 and after the
deductible in clause 4.1.

### 2.1 Collision

We cover damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision with another
vehicle, a person, an animal or a fixed object. This includes damage caused by
overturning or by leaving the road. We pay the cost of repair, or the market
value of the vehicle just before the loss if repair costs more than the vehicle
is worth.

### 2.2 Theft

We cover the loss of the insured vehicle through theft or attempted theft, and
damage caused to it during the theft. If the vehicle is not found within 28
days, we pay its market value just before the loss. If it is found damaged, we
pay the cost of repair.

### 2.3 Fire

We cover damage to or loss of the insured vehicle caused by fire, explosion,
lightning or a short circuit. We also pay the reasonable cost of moving the
vehicle after the fire and of removing the wreck.

### 2.4 Glass

We cover breakage of the windscreen, the side windows, the rear window and the
glass roof of the insured vehicle, whatever the cause. We pay for repair where
the glass can be repaired and for replacement where it cannot.

### 2.5 Storm

We cover damage to the insured vehicle caused by storm, hail or snow load, or by
trees, branches and roof parts blown onto it. A storm is a wind of at least
Beaufort force 8. We pay the cost of repair or, if repair is not possible, the
market value just before the loss.

### 2.6 Third-party liability

We pay the compensation that you, or a person driving with your permission, are
legally liable to pay for injury to other people or for damage to their property
caused by the use of the insured vehicle. We also pay the reasonable costs of
defending such a claim, if we agreed to them in advance. The limit in clause 4.2
applies to all claims that arise from one event.

## 3. What is not covered

The clauses below list what Motor Comprehensive does not cover. An exclusion
applies only to the claims that it names.

### 3.1 Racing

We do not cover loss or liability that arises while the insured vehicle takes
part in a race, rally, speed test, timed session or track day, or while it is
prepared for one, on or off a public road. This applies even if the event is
meant for amateurs or for driver training.

This exclusion applies to claims for collision.

### 3.2 Driving under the influence

We do not cover loss that occurs while the driver is under the influence of
alcohol, drugs or medicines that impair driving, or is above the legal limit of
the place where the loss happens. This also applies if the driver refuses a test
that the police ask for.

This exclusion applies to claims for collision.

### 3.3 Unlicensed driver

We do not cover loss that occurs while the insured vehicle is driven by a person
who does not hold a valid driving licence for that category of vehicle, or who
is banned from driving. This also applies if you knew that and let the person
drive.

This exclusion applies to claims for collision.

## 4. Deductible and limits

### 4.1 Deductible

You pay the first EUR 300 of every claim. We work out what we pay as the smaller
of the amount of the loss and the limit in clause 4.2, less the deductible. If
the loss is not more than the deductible, we pay nothing.

### 4.2 Limit

The most we pay for one claim is the sum insured shown on the schedule. If the
loss is larger, you bear the difference.

## 5. Making a claim

### 5.1 Reporting a claim

Report a claim to us within 30 days of the loss. Tell us what happened, when and
where, and send the documents that the clauses below list for the event. A
report that reaches us later does not by itself void your cover, but an adjuster
reviews the claim before we decide it.

### 5.2 Documents for collision

For a claim for collision we need the following documents.

- Photos (`photos`): clear photographs of the damage, taken before any repair or
  clearing up.
- Repair estimate (`repair_estimate`): a written estimate from a repairer or
  contractor that shows the cost.

We cannot decide the claim until these documents have reached us. We may ask for
more if what you send does not allow us to decide.

## 6. Period of cover

### 6.1 Period of cover

Cover runs from the start date to the end date shown on the schedule, both days
included. A loss that happens before the start date or after the end date falls
outside the period of cover. To stay covered after the end date you need to
renew the policy.

### 6.2 Lapse for non-payment

If a premium is not paid by its due date and stays unpaid after our written
reminder, the policy lapses. Cover ends on the lapse date recorded by us, so a
loss that happens on or after the lapse date is not covered. A loss that
happened before the lapse date stays covered.
